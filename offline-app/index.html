FREE TINNITUS HELP

About This App

Free Tinnitus Help was created from years of personal experience living with tinnitus and searching for a practical way to make it easier to manage.

Tinnitus is not the same for everyone. It may sound like ringing, whistling, buzzing, hissing, pulsing, electrical noise, or a combination of several sounds. That is why this app does not give everyone the same sound.

The app provides a broad library of customizable sounds so you can listen, adjust, and combine sound layers to create a mix that more closely resembles the tinnitus you hear.

You can explore frequency, bandwidth, texture, movement, volume, and listening time, then save a personalized mix for later use.

Why is it free? This project is provided free of charge as a charitable effort to help people living with tinnitus. No subscription. No paywall.

Our purpose is simple: give people with tinnitus more tools to explore their own sound and find an experience that may help them feel calmer, sleep better, or make tinnitus less intrusive.

Important: Free Tinnitus Help is a personal sound-matching and wellness tool. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose tinnitus, and does not promise to cure or treat any medical condition. Keep listening levels comfortable and stop if a sound causes discomfort. New, sudden, pulsatile, or one-sided tinnitus—especially with hearing loss or other symptoms—should be evaluated by a healthcare professional.

Safety & When to Get Medical Help · السلامة ومتى تطلب المساعدة الطبية

Listening safety
Use the lowest comfortable volume. The sound does not need to be loud. If listening causes discomfort, pain, dizziness, or noticeably worsens your tinnitus, stop playback and lower the level before trying again.
سلامة الاستماع
استخدم أقل مستوى صوت مريح. لا يحتاج الصوت إلى أن يكون مرتفعًا. إذا سبب الاستماع ألمًا أو انزعاجًا أو دوخة أو زيادة ملحوظة في الطنين، أوقف التشغيل وخفّض مستوى الصوت قبل المحاولة مرة أخرى.
Seek medical evaluation
New or sudden tinnitus, tinnitus that pulses with your heartbeat, or one-sided tinnitus accompanied by sudden hearing loss, marked dizziness, weakness, or other new neurological symptoms should not be managed only with this app.
اطلب تقييمًا طبيًا
الطنين الجديد أو المفاجئ، أو المتزامن مع نبض القلب، أو الطنين في جهة واحدة المصحوب بفقدان سمع مفاجئ أو دوخة شديدة أو ضعف أو أعراض عصبية جديدة، لا ينبغي الاعتماد على التطبيق وحده للتعامل معه.
